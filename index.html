Team access

Give a colleague access to the selected event. They create an account with the email below. A door staff member only sees the Check-in page. A treasurer only sees Contributions (can add and edit payments and expenses, but cannot delete or see guests). A photographer only sees the Photos page: he can upload and delete photos and copy the gallery link to send to clients (the host decides when the gallery is visible). A co-host (the event's host) can manage everything for this one event — guests, cards, messaging, check-in, contributions — but cannot delete the event, delete payment records or add team members. A co-host or photographer must register with a new email that does not already own events.

How sending works

WhatsApp

Messages are sent automatically when WhatsApp Business is connected; otherwise WhatsApp opens on your phone for each guest.

SMS

Sent through the SMS gateway; if it is not connected, your phone's SMS app opens for each guest.

Automatic reminders

One day before the event, guests who have not replied are reminded automatically (when automatic WhatsApp/SMS sending is connected).
